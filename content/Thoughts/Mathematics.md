Mathematics, at least the way that I found to be true for myself, has always been about assigning an understanding to the world in a quasi objetive language. While math are a construct of human imagination, there represent a universal language that, if it came to it, would be a medium in which alternate lifeforms would comprehend ( assuming intelligence, of course ).

Most of my math work is almost entirely **self study**, where I find interesting **papers**/**videos** on concepts, and write about them ( see the [[math]] tag ).

As far as work goes, I feign from handwritten solving due to my lack of proper handwriting skills. I write all my notes using LaTeX in obsidian here, and for formalizations I do everything in [[Lean Programming Language|Lean]] proofs.

My math background is that of a deep learning researcher, but because of neuroscience a lot of what I work with is [[Chaos, Fractals, and Dynamical Systems|Chaos Theory]] and a lot of Bayesian Probability. I am also dabbling in Group Theory now, for example the [[Continuous Attractor Network]] of a mouses brain can be modeled by the $SO(2)$ rotation group.

- experiment performed by medium of computation, in the case of biology/experimental sciences it is ones in which some process can be modeled and thus simulated within the computer.
- more recently has seen a redefinition for a new class of "digital twin" deep learning models, which can now serve an interesting use
	- one can imbue a sufficiently complex model with a [[Representation]] of some relationship: say that of a macaque [[Receptive Field]]/visual input to some source of brain activity information like a sharp [[Electrodes]] recording or [[fMRI]] activation data.
	- we make some assumptions that given these models are able to reliably predict brain activity given the associated stimuli, that they contain a substrate of the underlying representation that we ourselves have in the path from receptive field to information processing.
		- this isnt perfect, but that is part of the interesting experiment, as we get to measure divergences between model and human representations of media.

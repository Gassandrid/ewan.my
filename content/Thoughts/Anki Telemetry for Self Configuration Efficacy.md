So much of the stuff I have done here has been regarding [[Probing Nurture with Content Consumption|Probes]], but this might be one of the best ones yet.

The important idea is that the main mechanism behind what makes anki effective as a spaced repitition system is how it already leverages the telematry of how you rate cards you see to gauge your understanding, and uses a [[Neural Networks|Neural Network]] approach for this for the FSRS algorithm. Along with other things like [[Self Configuration For Chess]], could serve as a great testing ground for long term efficay of [[Neuropharmacology]] / [[Utilizing Cognition Enhancement Effectively]]

Has two signals: immediate working memory evaluation for seeing how quickly one learns new/errored cards during a session for a day, and then one for long term memory formation in how cards reviewed/accumulated over a few days are immediately recalled without prior practice for that session.

Also a good implementation of [[Consuming and Choosing Content Effectively]], especially when you can use coding agents to make cards as you go as defined by the [[Spaced Repetition for Learning Optimization|Spaced Repetition]] note.

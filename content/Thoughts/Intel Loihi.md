Neuromorphic chip made by Intel for running [[Spiking Neural Network]]s. I will likely have to learn the platform if I want to do work with [[Neuromorphic Computing]]. 

Has some very interesting features like **on chip learning** allowing for plasticity rules on a very low level for continuous adaptation. Also very very low power due to the event driven nature of [[Neuromorphic Computing]] platforms.

[[Sophia Sanborn]] has done some interesting work for efficient signal processing algorithms on the Loihi 2: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.03746

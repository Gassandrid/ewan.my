I have always been a fan of [[Philip Glass]], and he is by far my favorite composer. However I was unfamiliar with his older opera work in the form of **Einstein on the Beach**, which is one of his earliest accomplishments. 

Extremely esoteric and experimental, but that is exactly what I love about [[Philip Glass]] and his other collections like [[Koyaanisqatsi]] and [[Naqoyqatsi]].

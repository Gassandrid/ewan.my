an idea which I thought of as a very silly thought, in that "what if we combine [[tDCS and the Steerable Manifold|tDCS]] and compounds like [[TAK-653]]/[[ACD-856]]/[[AF710B]] to make ultra [[Long Term Potentiation]]!" 

turns out this is actually a subfield called pharmacological tDCS, where many papers [^1] have investigated this approach.

"pharmacological tdcs" is actually quite synergistic: tdcs devlivers a weak, sub threshold direct current that purely nudges resting [[Membrane Potential]]s without forcing any neurons to fire. Long term effects are completely defined by the brains baseline neurochemical state. by introducing [[AMPA Receptor#AMPA Positive Allosteric Modulators (sirsadalot)|positive allosteric modulators, such as for ampa]], or even dual receptors agonists along this electric field, we can lower the threshold for snypantic modification, converting the transient, weak eletrical polarization into a robust process for enduring [[Long Term Potentiation]]

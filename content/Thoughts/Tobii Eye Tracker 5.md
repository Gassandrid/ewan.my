The only device capable of tracking both head and eye movements for a more immersive gaming experience, content creation and simulation gaming.

Not a bad device, but Tobii is a ridiculous company. They make some of the best eye trackers, even better ones for research, but completely lock down the software/firmare for their cheaper gaming eye trackers. These are the only ones that I can plausibly afford, I have attempted to use camera arrays in addition with webcam experiments but the models are not quite there yet.

Enough for getting metrics like [[Saccadic Peak Eye Velocity]] but beyond that very locked down. It is capable of pupil dilation metrics but doesnt let you.

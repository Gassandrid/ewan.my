To understand a system is to see it under perturbation. A systems most ordinary behavior conceals the structure responsible, understanding only comes when looking for circumstances where where behavior changes, breaks down, or diverges from prediction. [[Merleau-Ponty]] discusses how neurological pathologies reveal the organization of embodied cogntion. [[Karl Popper]] notes importance of falsification as a source of knowledge, a sundial is best understood when it doesn't work without sun. [[Norbert Wiener]], through Cybernetics, exposes system organization through the disturbance-feedback cycle. Failure is the instrument of understanding, and the most important information about a system is often the least common.

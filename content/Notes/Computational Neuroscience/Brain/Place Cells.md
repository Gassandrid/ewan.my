- fires when navigating to specific locations,
- works with [[Grid Cells]] in the [[Entorhinal Cortex]] for tracking [[Spacial Navigation]]/distance/direction
	- more specifically works with [[Cognitive Map]] along with grid cells
- **place field:** each place cell assigned a specific preffered location know as this. entering the area means the corresponding place neurons fires
- **mental maps**: while you are moving ( see [[Theta Rhythm]] ), you see a unique changing combination of place cells that become active. 
	- entering a new environemtn will lead to the brain completely reorganizing its active cell patterns, this process is called *remapping*
		- https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.142105611
- beyond their navigation use cases, they are also implicated in evaluating future possibilities and for [[Sequence Learning]]/sequential organization so that memories dont overwrite one another.

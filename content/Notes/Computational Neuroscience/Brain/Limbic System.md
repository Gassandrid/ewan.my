[[Limbic System]] contains the [[Hippocampus]], [[Amygdala]], [[Hypothalamus]], and [[Cingulate Gyrus]], and [[Thalamus]]

- mainly responsible for managing emotion, memory, and basic survival behaviors. 
- sits underneath the [[Cerebral Cortex]] and above the [[Brainstem]]

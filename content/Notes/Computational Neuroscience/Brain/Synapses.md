specialized junction where [[Neuron]] passes and electrical/chem signal to another cell. in [[Computational Neuroscience]] we see them as dynamic, modifiable computational nodes actigng as sites for learning and memory.

- site of plasticity: physical substrates for [[Experience]] dependent plasticity. Efficicacy/weight is continually modified by the temporal correlation of [[Presynaptic Neurons|Presynaptic Neuron|presynaptic]] and [[Postsynaptic Neurons|postsynaptic]] acitivty, think [[Hebbian learning]] rules
- information transfer, act as filters/*optimal estimators* that process and relay signals while mediating energy constraints and statistical surprise.
- **probablistic integration** - instead of purely acting as static on/off switches, they use short term plasticity to continually adapt to chaning environmental uncertainties and task requirements.
	- [Synapses with short-term plasticity are optimal estimators of presynaptic membrane potentials](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3558743/)

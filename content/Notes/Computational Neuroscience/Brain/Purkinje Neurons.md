typically found in the [[Cerebellum]], they are massive, very branched [[Inhibition Neurons]]. 

- serve as the "sole output" channel of teh cerebellar cortex and play a large role in the job of motor coordination, balance, along with cognitive processing
- very distinctive pear shaped [[Soma]], and an extremely large [[Notes/Computational Neuroscience/Brain/Dendrites|Dendrite]] tree.
- each cell receives an enourmous number of inputs, with some going up to 200,000 parallel fiber [[Synapses]] from granule cells and a powerful input from a single climbing fiber
- they release [[GABA]] for surpressing/inhibiting the deep cerebellar nuclei.

Primary standardized method for applying patch [[Electrodes]] for [[On Doing EEG Work|EEG]]

- letter corresponds to Lobe/brain region it is reading from:
	- [[Prefrontal Cortex|Prefrontal]](Fp)
	- [[Frontal Lobe|Frontal]](F)
	- [[Temporal Lobe|Temporal]](T)
	- [[Parietal Lobe|Parietal]](P)
	- [[Occipital Lobe|Occipital]] (O)
	- Central (C)
		- *there is no "central lobe"; due to their placement, and depending on the individual, the "C" electrodes can exhibit/represent EEG activity more typical of frontal, temporal, and some parietal-occipital activity, and are always utilized in polysomnography sleep studies for the purpose of determining stages of sleep.* - wikipedia
	- also a Z site, refers to electrode placed on the midline/sagittal plane of the skull, (FpZ, Fz, Cz, Oz)
- numbers corresp to side
	- even numbers on left on right side, odd on left

- thin tube of nerve tissue running from base of brain down through center of spine
	- part of the [[Central Nervous System]], main control center of body
	- transmits both [[Afferent fibers|Afferent]] and [[Efferent fibers|efferent]] information, two way highway connecting [[Peripheral Nervous System]] to the brain

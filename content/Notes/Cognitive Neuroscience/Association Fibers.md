- most numerous kind of fiber within [[White Matter|white matter]]
- never crosses the midline: **intrahemispheric**
- two mains kinds, short u shaped **arcuate** fibers ( curved, shaped like a bow ), connect adjacent gyri. **long** fibers connect distant cortical areas

Five main groups:

1. Uncinate fasciculus: connects [[Frontal Lobe|frontal]] to [[Temporal Lobe|temporal lobe]]  
2. Superior longitudinal fasciculus: connects frontal, [[Occipital Lobe|occipital]], [[Parietal Lobe|parietal]], and temporal lobes  
3. [[Arcuate Fasciculus|Arcuate fasciculus]]: connects frontal and temporal lobes  
4. Inferior longitudinal fasciculus: connects occipital to temporal pole  
5. Cingulum: connects frontal and parietal lobes to para-[[Hippocampus|hippocampal]] gyrus and adjacent temporal gyri

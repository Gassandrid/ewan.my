- most numerous kind of fiber within white matter
- never crosses the midline: **intrahemispheric**
- two mains kinds, short u shaped **arcuate** fibers ( curved, shaped like a bow ), connect adjacent gyri. **long** fibers connect distant cortical areas

Five main groups:

1. Uncinate fasciculus: connects frontal to temporal lobe  
2. Superior longitudinal fasciculus: connects frontal, occipital, parietal, and temporal lobes  
3. Arcuate fasciculus: connects frontal and temporal lobes  
4. Inferior longitudinal fasciculus: connects occipital to temporal pole  
5. Cingulum: connects frontal and parietal lobes to para-hippocampal gyrus and adjacent temporal gyri

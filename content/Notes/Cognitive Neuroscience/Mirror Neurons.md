- fire both when someone performs an action and when someone observes another person performing said action. "mirrors" the behavior of the other as if the observer itself were actign
- some hypothises about mirror neurons being used for speech recognition
	- you yourself know the motor control of sounding out a word, but for things like the [[Cocktail Party Problem]], it could be helpful to imbue some motor representation of the other person speaking by their mouth movement you observe in your own decision process about recognizing what they say.

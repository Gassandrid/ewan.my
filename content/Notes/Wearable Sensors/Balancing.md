- **Center of mass** (CoM): Location of the whole body mass center. (a.k.a., “center of gravity”)  
- **Center of pressure** (CoP): Location of the net force perpendicular to gravity (for one foot or both)   
- **Base of support** (BoS): Range of possible locations of the CoP in a static posture.

**Static** balance: the abiltiy to keep the Center of Mass within the Base of Support

**What happens when the Center of Mass exceeds the Base of Support?**

**Dynamic** balance: the ability to keep the Center of Mass within the Base of Support over multiple gait cycles.

## Why Balance is a Complex Processo

- It requires sensory information, both instrinsic via body and extrinsic via environment info
	- proprioception ( body pos )
	- mechanoreception ( pressure )
	- visual
	- vestibular ( ears )
- **sensory integration** and **formation of a motor plan**
	- brain, cortical control
	- [[Spinal Cord]], sub cortical control
- **execution** of motor plan
	- neuromusculoskeletal ssytem
- young people dont tend to use a lot of concious cortical control, very subconcious, whereas for older people is a much more concious process.

## Ways to Balance

- **ankle strategy**
	- modulate your center of pressure within the foot/base of support
- **hip strategy**
	- modulate center of mass if center of pressure is limited ( e.g., balance beam )
	- arm motion has a similar effect
- **stepping**
	- when center of pressure exceeds base of support
	- additional center of mass modulation
